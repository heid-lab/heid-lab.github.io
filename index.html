What we do

Accelerating chemical discovery with AI

The Heid Lab is led by Esther Heid, Assistant Professor for Machine Learning for Sustainable Chemistry at TU Wien. We develop machine learning methods to predict and model chemical reactions, ranging from barrier heights and reaction energies to the geometries of transition states. Our work combines graph neural networks, generative models, and quantum chemistry.

Our research spans reaction property prediction, transition-state modeling, and ML-accelerated automated reaction pathway search across gas-phase, organocatalytic, and biocatalytic reactions.
