Grants

Our research is supported by competitive grants from the European Research Council and the Austrian Science Fund (FWF). Both awards were made in 2024 and fund the group's long-term work on machine learning for chemical reactions.

European Research Council · 2024

ERC Starting Grant

“Deep learning of chemical reactions” — generative and predictive deep-learning models for chemical reactions across gas-phase, organocatalytic, and biocatalytic systems.

Austrian Science Fund (FWF) · 2024

START Prize

The FWF's most highly endowed award for early-career researchers, supporting the group's long-term work on machine learning for sustainable chemistry.
