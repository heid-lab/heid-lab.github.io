ERC Starting Grant

“Deep learning of chemical reactions” — awarded to Esther Heid in September 2024 to develop generative and predictive deep-learning models for chemical reactions across gas-phase, organocatalytic, and biocatalytic systems.

The project

Predicting how a reaction will proceed — its products, its rate, its selectivity — remains one of the hardest problems in computational chemistry. The project builds models that learn reaction behavior directly from data while staying grounded in mechanism, so that predictions generalise beyond the reactions they were trained on.
